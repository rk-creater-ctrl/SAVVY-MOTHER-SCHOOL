Official Guidelines

Class-Wise Age Requirements (As of 31st March 2026)

Age calculations adhere to National Education Policy (NEP) and Madhya Pradesh State School Education norms.

Class / Grade Minimum Age Requirement Recommended Age Bracket Evaluation / Admission Mode
Toddler / Playgroup 1.5 Years 1.5 to 2.5 Years Informal Child-Parent Interaction
Nursery 2.5 Years 2.5 to 3.5 Years Elementary Activity & Color Recognition
Kindergarten I (LKG) 3.5 Years 3.5 to 4.5 Years Basic Interaction & Oral Assessment
Kindergarten II (UKG) 4.5 Years 4.5 to 5.5 Years Phonics & Basic Number Evaluation
Class 1 (Grade 1) 5.5 - 6 Years 5.5 to 6.5 Years Elementary Literacy & Numeracy Check
Classes 2 to 5 (Primary) Corresponding Age + Previous TC 6 to 10 Years Previous Class Report Card & TC
Classes 6 to 8 (Middle) Corresponding Age + Previous TC 11 to 13 Years Competency Assessment (Eng, Math, Sci)
Classes 9 & 10 (Secondary) Corresponding Age + Previous TC 14 to 15 Years Merit Assessment & Board Registration
Class 11 (Senior Secondary) Class 10 Board Passed 15 to 17 Years Class 10 Board Percentage & Stream Counseling

Senior Secondary Academic Streams

Savvy Mother International School provides specialized academic streams supported by modern labs and career counseling.

01

Science (PCM) - Engineering

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Computer Science / Physical Education. Ideal for engineering, robotics, architecture, and technology careers.

02

Science (PCB) - Medical

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Physical Education / Biotechnology. Tailored for medicine (NEET), biotechnology, pharmacy, and allied healthcare.

03

Commerce & Economics

Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English, Applied Mathematics / Informatics Practices. Designed for finance, management, and CA aspirants.

04

Humanities & Arts

History, Political Science, Geography / Economics, Psychology, English. Perfect for civil services (UPSC/MPPSC), law, journalism, and public policy.
