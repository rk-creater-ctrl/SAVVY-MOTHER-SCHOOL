Admission Process & Requirements

A transparent, stress-free admission procedure designed to welcome your family into our learning community.

Step-by-Step Guide

How To Secure Admission (Session 2026-27)

We believe in a parent-friendly and child-centric enrollment experience.

Step 01

Inquiry & Registration

Submit the online inquiry form or collect the prospectus from our campus admission office at Azad Nagar, Rewa. Our team will brief you on curriculum choices, fee structure, and school timings.

Step 02

Interactive Assessment

Pre-Primary (Toddler to KG): Friendly, stress-free interaction observing speech, color, and motor recognition.
Class 1 to 12: Elementary competency check in English, Mathematics, and Science to understand baseline learning.

Step 03

Document Verification

Submit the official admission kit along with student and parent identity documents, birth certificate, MP state SSSMID (Samagra ID), transfer certificate (TC), and previous marksheets.

Step 04

Confirmation & Welcome

Upon verification and completion of admission formalities, the enrollment number is generated. Parents receive the orientation schedule, uniform guidelines, and bus route allocation.

Document Checklist

Mandatory Documents for Admission

Please prepare self-attested copies of the following documents at the time of final enrollment:

📄 1. Birth Certificate

Original and photocopy issued by Municipal Corporation or competent authority (mandatory for Nursery to Class 1).

🆔 2. SSSMID (Samagra ID)

Family Samagra ID and Member ID for MP State educational records and portal compliance.

🪪 3. Aadhaar Cards

Clear copies of Student Aadhaar Card along with Father's and Mother's Aadhaar Cards.

🏫 4. Transfer Certificate (TC)

Original School Leaving / Transfer Certificate countersigned by education officer (applicable for Class 2 upwards).

📊 5. Academic Report Cards

Photocopy of previous year's report card / marksheet from the recognized school attended.

📸 6. Passport Photographs

6 recent color passport photographs of the student, and 2 photographs each of mother and father.

Fair & Inclusive

Our Admission Principles

At Savvy Mother International School, admissions are granted on a merit and first-come, first-served basis, strictly adhering to age criteria and availability of seats in each section.

We maintain an optimal student-to-teacher ratio to ensure that every learner receives personal attention, supportive guidance, and opportunities to flourish intellectually and socially.
