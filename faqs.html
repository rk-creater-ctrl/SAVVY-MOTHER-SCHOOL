Common Questions

  • Is day care or daycare available in Rewa?
    Yes, day care and daycare support is available at Savvy Mother International School Rewa with safe routines and attentive supervision.
  • Does the school offer kindergarten and nursery classes?
    Yes, the school offers nursery, Junior KG and Senior KG programs focused on early literacy, number sense, writing readiness, confidence and classroom habits.
  • Can parents apply for preschool admission in Rewa?
    Parents can contact the school office or use the admission form for preschool, day care, daycare, nursery, KG and primary admission guidance.
  • Is transport available?
    Transport depends on route availability. Please confirm your location with the office.
  • Are meals provided?
    The school provides fresh and hot lunch support as per the current facility plan.
  • How are parents updated?
    Parents are informed through school communication, phone calls and parent-teacher interactions.
  • Can we visit the campus?
    Yes, school visits can be planned during working hours after contacting the office.
