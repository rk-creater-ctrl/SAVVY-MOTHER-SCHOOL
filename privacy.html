Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting the privacy, safety, and digital confidentiality of students and parents.

1. Information We Collect

Savvy Mother International School collects personal information submitted during admission inquiries, application submissions, and portal registration. This includes student names, dates of birth, parent contact numbers, email addresses, residential locations, and medical health disclosures required for student safety.

2. Use of Information

All collected data is strictly utilized for academic administration, emergency notifications, parent communications, student performance tracking via the ReskooleR portal, and compliance with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and State Education Department regulations. We never sell, rent, or monetize student or parent data.

3. Photographs & Media Consent

Photographs and videos from school assemblies, cultural events, sports meets, and classroom achievements may be published in official school newsletters, prospectuses, and official website galleries. Parents wishing to opt out of media publishing for their ward may notify the school administration office in writing.

4. Portal & Data Security

Our digital databases and ReskooleR ERP portal employ industry-standard encryption protocols. Parents are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their portal passwords and reporting any unauthorized access immediately.

5. Contact Our Data Desk

If you have any questions regarding our privacy framework, please email us at savvymotherschool@gmail.com or visit our administrative office at 16/1034, Azad Nagar, Ward 16, Urrahat, Rewa, MP — 486001.
