World-Class Infrastructure & Facilities

A thoughtfully architected learning environment equipped with modern digital tools, scientific laboratories, creative studios, and expansive sports amenities.

A Campus Designed for Curious Minds

Spaces That Inspire Innovation and Well-Being

At Savvy Mother International School, our infrastructure is built to support 21st-century experiential learning. From interactive smart classrooms to dedicated robotics workshops and open playfields, every space has been carefully crafted to make school days joyful, safe, and stimulating.

We adhere strictly to safety protocols, high air quality, hygienic dining, clean RO drinking water stations, and complete CCTV surveillance across every corridor and learning zone.

Campus Pillars

Core Facilities at a Glance

Designed to nurture academic rigor, physical health, artistic expression, and emotional security.

Digital Learning

Smart Interactive Classrooms

Touch-enabled digital boards, interactive 3D learning modules, audio-visual lessons, and ergonomic furniture.

STEM & Innovation

Robotics & AI Innovation Lab

Hands-on microcontrollers, sensor kits, Scratch & Python coding, and competitive robotics team prep.

Scientific Inquiry

Science Laboratories

Dedicated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology lab apparatus with highest safety protocols for practical experiments.

Information Tech

Computer & Coding Hub

High-speed fiber connectivity, modern workstations, cyber safety curriculum, and computational logic classes.

Literacy & Research

Knowledge Hub & Library

Curated collection of 10,000+ fiction, non-fiction, encyclopedias, journals, and a peaceful reading sanctuary.

Athletics & Physical Ed

Sports Arena & Turf

Basketball court, cricket practice nets, football pitch, indoor badminton, table tennis, and yoga lawn.

Safety & Commute

GPS-Enabled Safe Transport

Fleet of well-maintained buses covering all Rewa zones, fitted with CCTV, speed governors, and verified female attendants.

Care & Well-being

Infirmary & Wellness Care

Trained medical staff on duty, first-aid support, routine pediatric dental/eye check-ups, and student counseling.

Peace of Mind

Our Campus Safety Standards

We believe learning flourishes only where children feel completely safe, protected, and respected.

01

24/7 CCTV & Security

Comprehensive camera coverage across entry gates, corridors, playgrounds, and buses monitored continuously by dedicated security personnel.

02

Pure RO Drinking Water

Multi-stage reverse osmosis filtration systems with regular laboratory quality testing across all student drinking stations.

03

Clean & Sanitized Restrooms

Age-separated, hygienically maintained washrooms with continuous cleaning staff and eco-friendly sanitary disposal systems.
