Senior Secondary Wing (Classes 11 & 12)

Specialized academic streams, competitive examination coaching integration, and university pathway counseling.

Classes 11 & 12 · Specialized Academic Streams

Excellence That Nurtures the Whole Child

The Senior Secondary stage at Savvy Mother International School is the launchpad for higher education and professional careers. We offer four distinct, comprehensive academic streams taught by veteran post-graduate faculty who combine syllabus rigor with competitive exam guidance.

Whether a student aspires to enter top engineering colleges (JEE), medical universities (NEET), chartered accountancy (CA/CS), civil services (UPSC/MPPSC), law (CLAT), or design, our program integrates conceptual depth, competitive mock tests, and university application counseling.

● Conceptual · Experiential · Empowering

Curricular Highlights

How Learning Happens at This Stage

Every classroom activity is designed to spark intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and collaborative problem-solving.

01

Competitive Exam Integration

Structured guidance for competitive exams alongside the board curriculum, including regular problem-solving sessions.

02

Career & University Counseling

One-on-one counseling identifying student strengths, psychometric profiling, college entrance strategies, and scholarship guidance.

03

State-of-the-Art Research Labs

Advanced laboratory equipment allowing students to conduct sophisticated experiments and investigatory project work.

04

Holistic Ethics & Poise

Equipping young adults with emotional intelligence, ethical decision-making, presentation skills, and resilience for adulthood.

Academic Progression

Grade Offerings & Structure

Science Stream (PCM)

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Computer Science / Physical Education for engineering & tech.

Science Stream (PCB)

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Physical Education / Biotechnology for medicine, healthcare & research.

Commerce Stream

Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English, Applied Mathematics / Informatics Practices for finance & business.

Humanities Stream

History, Political Science, Economics, Psychology / Geography, English for civil services, law, media & humanities.
