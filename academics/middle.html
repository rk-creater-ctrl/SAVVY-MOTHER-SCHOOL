Middle School (Preparatory & Middle Stage)

Transitioning from fundamental learning into inquiry-based science, analytical thinking, and self-discipline.

Classes 6 to 8 · Ages 11 to 14 Years

Excellence That Nurtures the Whole Child

Middle school marks an exciting developmental transition where children move from concrete understanding into abstract logic and critical questioning. At Savvy Mother International School, our middle school curriculum bridges foundational knowledge with experimental learning.

Students explore comprehensive curricula spanning English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Integrated Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Sciences, and Computer Coding. We integrate Abacus mental math training and Spoken English debate sessions to ensure strong analytical and communication skills.

● Conceptual · Experiential · Empowering

Curricular Highlights

How Learning Happens at This Stage

Every classroom activity is designed to spark intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and collaborative problem-solving.

01

Inquiry-Based Science Labs

Hands-on lab investigations where concepts of magnetism, chemical reactions, optics, and cell structures are tested practically.

02

Abacus & Mental Math

Specialized mental calculation techniques that enhance cognitive speed, focus, spatial memory, and number agility.

03

Robotics & Coding Basics

Early introduction to algorithmic logic, block-based programming (Scratch), and introductory sensor-based robotics.

04

Public Speaking & Literary Club

Debating, elocution, creative writing, and drama circles that develop persuasive eloquence and articulate communication.

Academic Progression

Grade Offerings & Structure

Class 6: The Foundation Year

Adapting to specialist subject teachers, independent study habits, project work, and lab safety.

Class 7: Deepening Inquiries

Algebraic reasoning, world geography, practical science experiments, and inter-house debates.

Class 8: The Pre-Board Gateway

Advanced conceptual mastery, scientific methodology, introductory computer programming, and career exploration.
