Holi & Rangotsav

Celebrating the colors of spring with eco-friendly herbal gulal, floral traditions, and brotherhood.

Colors of Spring & Cultural Harmony

Eco-Friendly Rangotsav & Joyous Springtide

Holi, the festival of colors, is celebrated at Savvy Mother International School with supreme elegance, safety, and cultural warmth. Welcoming the vibrant season of spring (Vasant Ritu), Rangotsav symbolizes the triumph of righteousness, forgiveness, and the renewal of friendships.

Championing environmental consciousness, Savvy Mother celebrates an exemplary Eco-Friendly Herbal & Floral Holi. Students use 100% organic herbal gulal derived from marigold petals, rose leaves, and natural turmeric. With festive folk songs, dholak beats, and traditional sweets like gujiya, the celebration instills cultural pride while honoring water conservation.

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate Rangotsav

Spreading joy, fostering mutual respect, and teaching ecological responsibility through celebration.

01

100% Organic Herbal Gulal

Certified skin-friendly and safe organic dry colors prepared from marigold flowers, spinach, and beetroots ensuring student wellness.

02

Floral Phoolon Ki Holi

A mesmerizing traditional celebration where students and teachers shower fragrant rose and marigold petals in joyous harmony.

03

Spring Folk Music & Dance

Students present lively classical and folk dance recitals on traditional Holi geet, accompanied by harmonium and daphli.

04

Water Conservation Pledge

Special morning assembly where students take a solemn oath to celebrate waterless dry Holi, preserving our city's precious resources.
