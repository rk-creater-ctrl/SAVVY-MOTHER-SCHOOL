International Yoga Day

Harmonizing body, mind, and breath through ancient yogic wisdom and practice.

Mindfulness & Physical Wellness

Joyful Moments, Lifelong Values

Observed annually on June 21st, International Yoga Day at Savvy Mother International School is a mass celebration of healthy living, inner tranquility, and holistic wellness. In the early morning hours, our expansive school grounds are covered with yoga mats.

Under the guidance of certified yoga instructors, students from pre-primary to senior secondary along with faculty members execute synchronized Surya Namaskars, pranayama breathing exercises, and meditation techniques designed to enhance academic focus, memory, and emotional poise.

● Joy · Culture · Character

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate International Yoga Day

Every activity is thoughtfully curated to inspire teamwork, confidence, and cultural appreciation.

01

Mass Surya Namaskar Protocol

Hundreds of students performing the 12-step Sun Salutation in absolute synchrony to soothing Sanskrit chants.

02

Pranayama for Student Focus

Workshops teaching Anulom-Vilom, Bhramari, and Kapalbhati to relieve exam anxiety and enhance mental clarity.

03

Acrobatic & Rhythmic Yoga

Demonstrations by our school sports team exhibiting advanced postures (Chakrasana, Bakasana, Halasana).

04

Holistic Health Seminar

Interactive session with health experts on balanced nutrition, posture correction, and digital eye strain reduction.
