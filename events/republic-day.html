Republic Day

Saluting the Constitution of India with disciplined march-pasts, cultural tableaus, and patriotic pride.

Sovereign Republic & Democratic Heritage

Honouring Our Nation, Constitution, and Heroes

Every 26th of January, Savvy Mother International School transforms into a grand arena of patriotic vigor and unity. Republic Day marks the historic date when the Constitution of India came into effect in 1950, enshrining sovereign rights, democracy, justice, and secular brotherhood across our vast motherland.

The celebrations begin at sunrise with the ceremonial unfurling of the Indian Tricolour by the School Director and Principal, followed by the high-spirited National Anthem. Students from all four school houses step in synchronized harmony for the ceremonial parade, accompanied by our school brass band, patriotic hymns, and cultural representations of India's states.

● Constitution · Unity · Democracy

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate Republic Day

Instilling civic consciousness, national pride, and respect for our constitutional values.

01

Tricolour Unfurling & Salute

The National Flag is hoisted amidst the resonance of the National Anthem, followed by floral salute and tri-color dove release.

02

Grand House March-Past

House contingents led by the Head Boy, Head Girl, and House Captains march with crisp coordination and discipline.

03

Constitutional Preamble Oath

A solemn community pledge taken by all students and teachers to uphold justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity for every citizen.

04

Cultural Tableau & Folk Dance

Vibrant state-themed presentations portraying India's multifaceted culture, scientific innovations, and freedom fighters.
