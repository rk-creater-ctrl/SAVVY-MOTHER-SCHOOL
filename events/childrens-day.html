Children's Day (Bal Diwas)

A joyous day dedicated entirely to honoring our students with fun fairs, teacher shows, games, and pure happiness.

Joyful Childhood & Boundless Potential

Celebrating the Heart and Soul of Our School

Every 14th of November, Savvy Mother International School celebrates Children's Day (Bal Diwas) in commemoration of Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru's birth anniversary. It is a day where the normal timetable is joyfully suspended, and the entire campus turns into an extraordinary carnival of games, stage shows, laughter, and camaraderie.

On this unique day, the teachers take over the morning assembly—acting out comic sketches, singing student favorites, and presenting lively musical dances to bring radiant smiles to every student's face. From toddler play areas to middle and senior school game tournaments, Bal Diwas is a cherished milestone where children feel genuinely treasured and inspired.

● Joy · Laughter · Inspiration

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate Bal Diwas

Special experiences designed so every student feels celebrated, valued, and free to express their joyful nature.

01

Teachers' Special Stage Assembly

Faculty members enthusiastically step on stage to perform parodies, musical recitals, and entertaining skits prepared exclusively for their students.

02

School Joy Fair & Games Carnival

Interactive carnival stalls featuring ring toss, bowling alleys, puzzle races, creative face painting, and magic show tents across the school lawn.

03

Student Open-Mic & Talent Arena

An encouraging, non-judgmental open platform where students share their poems, jokes, guitar tunes, or impromptu dance routines.

04

Treats & Keepsake Distribution

Every child is gifted special souvenir badges, balloons, chocolates, and personalized handwritten cards from their class teachers.
