Diwali Celebration

Spreading warmth, artistic rangolis, and compassionate giving across our community.

Festival of Lights & Harmony

Joyful Moments, Lifelong Values

Deepotsav at Savvy Mother International School is a luminous reflection of joy, wisdom, and fellowship. The school corridors come alive with radiant floral rangolis, hand-painted terracotta diyas, and lanterns handcrafted by our students.

We place special emphasis on 'Green Diwali'—educating students on celebrating without harmful chemical crackers. The festival also marks our annual 'Joy of Giving' drive, where students assemble books, stationary, clothes, and warm blankets for underprivileged families in Rewa.

● Joy · Culture · Character

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate Diwali Celebration

Every activity is thoughtfully curated to inspire teamwork, confidence, and cultural appreciation.

01

Inter-House Rangoli Fiesta

A vibrant competition where houses craft intricate designs using organic colors, flower petals, and pulses.

02

Terracotta Diya Painting

Art workshops where students paint traditional clay lamps to take home and illuminate their family spaces.

03

Joy of Giving Outreach

Community service project distributing gift hampers and educational kits to needy communities.

04

Green Diwali Awareness

Student street plays educating the public about noise reduction, air preservation, and animal safety during Diwali.
