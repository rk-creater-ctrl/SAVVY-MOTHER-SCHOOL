Book Week & Reading Carnival

Fostering curiosity, vocabulary, and timeless imagination through books.

Celebrating the Magic of Words

Joyful Moments, Lifelong Values

Book Week at Savvy Mother International School transforms our library and campus into a vibrant literary wonderland. Designed to cultivate a lifelong love for reading, this week-long festival engages students in author read-alouds, book fairs, and creative storytelling.

Students explore diverse genres spanning Indian mythology, historical fiction, world classics, science, and poetry. The week culminates in our famed 'Literary Character Parade', where students dress up as their favorite book characters and recite famous literary dialogues.

● Joy · Culture · Character

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate Book Week & Reading Carnival

Every activity is thoughtfully curated to inspire teamwork, confidence, and cultural appreciation.

01

Literary Costume Parade

Students bring their favorite heroes—from Harry Potter and Swami to Sherlock Holmes and Tenali Rama—to life on stage.

02

Grand Book Exhibition

Leading regional and national publishers exhibit thousands of titles in our auditorium for students and parents.

03

Story Weaver & Poetry Slam

Competitions where students write and recite their own short stories, ballads, and spoken-word poetry.

04

Book Donation 'Drop Everything & Read'

A campus-wide silent reading hour followed by donating books to create classroom micro-libraries.
