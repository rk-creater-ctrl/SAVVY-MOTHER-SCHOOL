Christmas Celebration

Spreading harmony, joyful carols, and community warmth during the winter season.

Season of Giving & Goodwill

Joyful Moments, Lifelong Values

Christmas at Savvy Mother International School is an enchanting celebration of peace, universal love, and generosity. The campus sparkles with festive decorations, handcrafted paper bells, mistletoe, and a majestic Christmas tree decorated by pre-primary and primary students.

Students of all faiths come together to sing timeless carols, understand the virtues of compassion, and participate in nativity enactments. A cheerful surprise visit from Santa Claus brings laughter, candies, and festive excitement to our youngest learners.

● Joy · Culture · Character

Event Highlights

How We Celebrate Christmas Celebration

Every activity is thoughtfully curated to inspire teamwork, confidence, and cultural appreciation.

01

Nativity Skit & Carols

The school choir presents melodious renditions of 'Silent Night' and 'Jingle Bells' alongside a scenic nativity play.

02

Grand Christmas Tree Decoration

Students create eco-friendly origami ornaments, stars, and handwritten wishes for the community tree.

03

Santa's Surprise Arrival

Santa Claus visits classrooms distributing toffees, goodie bags, and spreading boundless holiday cheer.

04

Secret Santa Kindness Circle

Students exchange secret letters of encouragement, homemade cookies, and thoughtful tokens of friendship.
